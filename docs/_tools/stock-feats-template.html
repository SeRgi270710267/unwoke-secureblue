Shipped first

We watch official secureblue issues. Every ticket we shipped first stays on this page. Open ones stay until they ship. When they ship, the card moves and says they shipped after us. If we later copy their better patch, that is written here on purpose.

[FEAT] is a request, not a release. On their tracker that prefix means “please add this.” An open ticket means they have not shipped it. We already did. Closed after our date means they shipped later. Not affiliated. We do not auto-copy their patch.

Checking last green…
__AHEAD_N__still ahead of stock
__AFTER_N__they shipped after us
__OLDEST__longest wait still open

What this list is not

We do not ship every request on their tracker. UKI, ARM, server images, kernel modules, TPM, and SELinux policy need their signed base — an overlay cannot honestly claim those. We also skip FEATs that would weaken the box (Safe Browsing off, unloading all audio, default system Flatpaks). Bugs that hit this overlay belong on Workarounds, not here.
