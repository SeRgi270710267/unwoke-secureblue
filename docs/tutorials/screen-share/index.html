Meetings

Screen share

Keep locked Extra sandbox pack: audio sandbox on, screen capture off, JS optimizer off. Xwayland is still off (stock). Wayland portals are the path, not “enable X11 for Zoom.”

  1. Try the portal first

    GNOME/KDE picker for a window or display. If the site or Flatpak never prompts, it is often policy, not a missing pipewire package.

  2. Loosen only the sandbox pack

    ujust set-brave-sandbox off

    Restart the house browser. Do not turn isolation off (WebGL/WebGPU) or JIT on for a meeting. Do not turn Xwayland on unless their FAQ says that exact app is X-only — and treat that app as untrusted.

Related: camera and mic. Bubblejail on Origin can also break PipeWire/portals — ujust why option for GPU/audio, or ujust set-brave-bubblejail off.
