Unwoke SecureBlue

A security-focused Fedora Atomic desktop: secureblue’s hardening, Brave Origin or no browser, no curator store. Unwoke look by default.

Get Unwoke SecureBlue

Not affiliated with secureblue. This is a personal overlay on their signed images. Stock secureblue is at secureblue.dev.

About

Unwoke SecureBlue is a daily BlueBuild overlay on official secureblue Fedora Atomic images. Kernel hardening, SELinux, hardened_malloc, closed firewall, and their ujust commands stay. We only change two product decisions: the house browser and the house app store.

Default images ship the standalone Brave Origin RPM (brave-origin), not full brave-browser and not Trivalent. Browserless images (*-browserless) strip Trivalent and install nothing in its place: no Origin, no brave_t, stock harden_userns. There is no GUI software store and no Flathub remote until ujust set-flathub verified. Every extra lock has a ujust to turn it off.

It is shipped as OCI bootable container images. Rebase onto one of them from secureblue or any Fedora Atomic desktop. For details, see the features list.

Their hardening

Kernel args, SELinux, hardened_malloc, no Xwayland by default, USBGuard, run0. Inherited, not reimplemented. Unwoke navy wallpaper and blue accent on GNOME and KDE.

Brave Origin

Default flavor. Standalone RPM. Not Trivalent, not full Brave. Optional -browserless images have no browser at all.

No curator store

Bazaar, GNOME Software, and Plasma Discover are gone. Use the terminal: flatpak, brew, rpm-ostree.

Unwoke look

Navy wallpaper, lock screen, dark style, blue accent. Every extra lock is a ujust. See Brand and toggles.

Who this is for

People who already want Linux, already want secureblue’s hardening, and do not want Trivalent or a curated app catalog deciding which browsers exist.

Origin images are not as locked down as stock secureblue + Trivalent. Browserless is tighter than Origin until you install a browser; that install is blocked until ujust set-allow-browsers on ALLOW. It is not a Trivalent replacement. If maximum desktop confinement and a web browser is your first priority, use stock secureblue.

Honest limits

  • Brave Origin is not Trivalent. No Vanadium-style Chromium patches, no tight Trivalent SELinux jail.
  • On Origin images, brave_t is an unconfined-like domain on the userns allow-list. Browserless does not add that exception.
  • The signing key is a GitHub secret, not a hardware key.
  • We do not rebuild their kernel or re-run their SLSA pipeline. We inherit whatever they already shipped, after checking their signature.
