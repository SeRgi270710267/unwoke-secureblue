Images

Published as ghcr.io/sergi270710267/<name>:latest. All are public. No GitHub login to pull. Rebuilt twice a day, 08:00 and 20:00 UTC, plus every recipe push. No suffix = Brave Origin. Names ending in -trivalent keep stock Trivalent plus extra policies. Names ending in -browserless have no image browser. Every image gets the Unwoke wallpaper, lock screen, and blue accent, Flathub/brew/Bluetooth/toolbox off until ujust, and the other overlay locks. Reverts: Post-install.

nvidia-open images are for NVIDIA GPUs Turing or newer (GTX 16xx / RTX 20xx and later): proprietary driver with NVIDIA’s open kernel modules, not Nouveau. The non-NVIDIA images use Nouveau and are a poor choice for those GPUs.

What they sit on

Each overlay is built from the matching official secureblue image, after cosign verify with their public key:

  • ghcr.io/secureblue/silverblue-main-hardened
  • ghcr.io/secureblue/silverblue-nvidia-open-hardened
  • ghcr.io/secureblue/kinoite-main-hardened
  • ghcr.io/secureblue/kinoite-nvidia-open-hardened

No Sway, COSMIC, CoreOS, or IoT images here. Those stay on their image list.

Verify

cosign verify --key cosign.pub ghcr.io/sergi270710267/unwoke-silverblue

cosign.pub is in the git repo. After the first-boot signed rebase, rpm-ostree checks that stamp on updates.
