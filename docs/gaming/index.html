Vs stock secureblue · vs CachyOS

Play the match. Then the box locks itself again.

Stock will let you game. The problem is what happens after. Their Steam install turns on Xwayland or anti-cheat and leaves those holes open until you remember to close them. Unwoke asks once, then a play window borrows CachyOS’s session trick — GameMode while the title runs — and puts every overlay lock back when you quit. Crash or reboot: the leftover stamp is a fail, not a quiet weaker machine.

Restores No kernel swap Nothing silent

One sentence. Same signed secureblue kernel. Faster governors for the match. Full Unwoke the moment Steam exits. We do not become CachyOS. We do not stay as loose as stock.

Three rooms

01 · Stock

They loosen and forget

Need Xwayland? ujust set-xwayland on. Need Easy Anti-Cheat? ujust set-ptrace on. Tomorrow you are in the browser with ptrace still allowed. There is no “the game is over.”

02 · CachyOS

They rebuild the whole OS for FPS

Custom kernel, packages compiled for your CPU, Proton with extra patches. That is a different, faster computer. It is not a signed secureblue ostree. You cannot overlay it and still say “same foundation.”

03 · Unwoke

A window, not a second OS

Setup → Gaming, or ujust play steam. Fedora GameMode raises CPU/GPU governors for that process. Close Steam: a watcher restores. Optional asked sched-ext is the closest CachyOS switch without their kernel. SMT and Spectre mitigations stay.

The leftover FPS gap is their kernel and rebuilt packages. Everything else that can be a session is automatic. Click Steam. Close Steam.

On the machine

ujust install-steam
# then just click Steam in the app grid
# play
# close Steam — restore is automatic

No extra command to “turn it off.” A login agent sees Steam start and quit. ujust play stop still exists if you want it. Healthy later: ujust unwoke-test says PASS no play window. How-to: Play window tutorial.

What this page does not claim

We will not beat CachyOS at raw FPS on a weak GPU. Their rebuilt mesa and kernel are real. We will not turn off Spectre mitigations “just for the game.” We will not silent-enable ptrace. The sell is simpler: you can play, and you do not stay open.
