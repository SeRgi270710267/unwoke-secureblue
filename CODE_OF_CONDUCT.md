# Conduct

This is **not** the Contributor Covenant. Stock [secureblue](https://secureblue.dev/code-of-conduct) ships that (v2.0, identity checklist, Mozilla-style four-step ban ladder). Unwoke SecureBlue does not. Full page: https://sergi270710267.github.io/unwoke-secureblue/conduct/

## Rules

1. Don't make the maintainer invent more rules.
2. If you do, you are gone, and the extra rule can go with you after a cooldown.
3. If you are a bigger pain than your contributions justify, you are ejected.

Also: no malware, no doxxing, no spam. Technical disagreement is fine. Demanding we adopt their Covenant is not a contribution.

## Scope

This repository. Not your timeline.

## Enforcement

The maintainer. No committee. GitHub's ToS still apply.
