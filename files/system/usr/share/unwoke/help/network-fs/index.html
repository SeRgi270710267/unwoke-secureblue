NAS

Network shares (NFS/CIFS)

Keep locked Stock already masks the NFS server. Unwoke also blacklists NFS and CIFS client modules so a random share does not mount. That is stricter than stock.

  1. Need a NAS?

    ujust set-network-fs on

    Then you may need run0 modprobe cifs or nfs. This is a security loosen. Put it back when you are done.

  2. Done with the share

    ujust set-network-fs off

    Unmount first. Already-mounted shares stay until you unmount them.
