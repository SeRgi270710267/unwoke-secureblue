Fingerprinting vs locks

We ship maximum security first. Extra browser packs make you rarer than stock Trivalent. Phone-home off (countme, connectivity, DHCP hostname, thumbnails) does not fingerprint websites — leave those off.

Best crowd on Unwoke: *-trivalent. Origin is a smaller crowd.

If you choose to look closer to stock Trivalent (one pack, then restart the browser):

ujust set-brave-jitless off
ujust set-brave-isolation off
ujust set-brave-devices off

Do not flatten every pack. Do not install an “anti-detect” browser. Do not Bubblejail Trivalent.
